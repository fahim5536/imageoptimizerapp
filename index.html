How to Use

Get started in minutes — no technical knowledge needed.

01 Download & Install

Download the .exe file, follow the setup wizard and launch Image Optimizer Pro from the desktop shortcut.

02 Add Your Images

Drag and drop one image, hundreds of images or a complete folder into the drop zone.

03 Choose Settings

Select the output format, quality and optional width or height while keeping your files local.

04 Process & Save

Click optimize, review the results and download your lighter, polished files.

💡 WebP is a great default for web images.
